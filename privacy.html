Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains what data Pixel Perdido Publisher (the "App") handles. The App is a private tool used only by the owner of the Pixel Perdido channel to upload the channel's own videos to the owner's TikTok account as drafts.

1. Data we receive from TikTok

  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok when the owner authorizes the App, used only to call the TikTok API on the owner's behalf.
  • Basic account information (open ID, display name and avatar), used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Upload status returned by TikTok for each video (for example, whether the draft was received).

The App does not access followers, messages, comments, analytics or any data of other TikTok users.

2. How we use it

Only to upload Pixel Perdido videos to the owner's TikTok drafts and to show the upload result to the owner. We do not use the data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

Tokens are stored on the channel's private production server, in a file readable only by the App's system user, and are never published, logged in plain text or shared. Videos and their texts are stored on the same server while they are being produced and reviewed.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok, through TikTok's official API, to perform the upload requested by the owner.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the connection is active and are deleted when access is revoked or on request. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions). Deletion requests can be sent through our contact channel and are handled within 30 days.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children and is used only by the adult owner of the channel.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date at the top shows the latest version.

8. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: github.com/eugustavokeller/pixelperdido/issues.
