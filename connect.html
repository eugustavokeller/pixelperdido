Connect your TikTok account

Private tool of the Pixel Perdido channel. Only the channel owner uses it.

What happens when you connect
  1. You sign in with TikTok and authorize Pixel Perdido Publisher (scopes: user.info.basic, video.upload).
  2. When you approve a finished Pixel Perdido video in our review panel, our server sends it to your TikTok inbox as a draft.
  3. You open TikTok, review the draft, add the caption and publish it yourself.

We never publish automatically and we only read your display name and avatar to show which account is connected.

By connecting you agree to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. You can revoke access anytime in TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions.
