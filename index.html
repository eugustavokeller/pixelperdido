Pixel Perdido

Short videos about forgotten stories from history, games and technology.

Pixel Perdido is an independent content channel that publishes short vertical videos (YouTube Shorts and TikTok). Each video is researched, written and animated for the channel.

Pixel Perdido Publisher

Pixel Perdido Publisher is the channel's private publishing tool. It is used only by the channel owner to send finished videos from our production server to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts, which the owner then reviews and publishes manually in the TikTok app. It does not offer accounts or services to other people.
